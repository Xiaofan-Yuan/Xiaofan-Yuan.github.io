About Me

Before joining Arizona State University in August 2022, I obtained my Ph.D. in the multidisciplinary program Algorithms, Combinatorics and Optimization (ACO) at the School of Mathematics, Georgia Institute of Technology, under the supervision of Professor Xingxing Yu.

I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from University of Science and Technology of China in 2017. My undergraduate research advisor is Professor Jie Ma.

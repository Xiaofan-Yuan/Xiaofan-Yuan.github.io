About Me

I joined the Institute for Basic Science in January 2026. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences at Arizona State University from August 2022 to December 2025.

I obtained my Ph.D. in the multidisciplinary program Algorithms, Combinatorics, and Optimization (ACO) from the School of Mathematics at Georgia Institute of Technology in August 2022, under the supervision of Professor Xingxing Yu. I received my B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Science and Technology of China in June 2017. My undergraduate research advisor was Professor Jie Ma.
